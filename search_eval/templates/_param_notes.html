qf replaces the fields searched. It doesn't reweight production's fields: qf=title^2 searches titles only, and on "triton atmosphere" it matches 157 records instead of 1,127. To change one weight, start from production's own and edit it:

{{ config['PRODUCTION_DEFAULT_QF'] }}

A boost you pass is applied twice, and cite_read_boost stays in. On production the query becomes your boost × (cite_read_boost + 0.5) × your boost again, so pow(citation_count,0.1) acts like an exponent of 0.2. There is no way yet to replace cite_read_boost through this parameter.
